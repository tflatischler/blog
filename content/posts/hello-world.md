Hi there. I'm Tim, a 15-year-old student from Eisenstadt, Austria, and this is my little corner of the internet.

By day I'm a Gymnasium student working toward my Matura. By night (and honestly, also by day) I'm a maker, tinkerer, and self-taught developer who can't stop building things.

## What I'm into

**Homelabbing** is probably my biggest hobby right now. I run a home server called *Chuck Norris*: an AMD Threadripper machine with dual RTX 3090s and 128GB of RAM — that hosts everything from Gitea and Nextcloud to my own AI inference stack via Ollama. I manage the whole thing through Docker and spend way too much time optimising it.

**AI & LLMs** fascinate me on both a technical and philosophical level. I'm currently planning my VWA (a pre-scientific thesis required for the Austrian Matura) on the topic of functional emotions in large language models. I'm basically asking: do AI systems have something *like* feelings, even if not the real thing?

**Web development** is something I'm actively learning through freeCodeCamp. HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Node.js — I'm building up from the basics, and this blog is one of my first real projects.

**Filmmaking** is another side of me. I shoot on a Sony A6600 with anamorphic lenses and edit in DaVinci Resolve. I love the visual storytelling side of it just as much as the technical gear side.

**3D printing & CAD** round things out — I design parts in Fusion 360 and print them on my Phrozen Sonic Mighty 8K resin printer. I once designed an entire custom camera lens assembly from scratch, which was a wild project.

## Why this blog?

I wanted a place to document what I'm working on, what I'm learning, and occasionally what breaks spectacularly. Expect posts about homelab setups, AI experiments, dev projects, and maybe the odd filmmaking note.

If any of that sounds interesting — stick around.

— Tim
